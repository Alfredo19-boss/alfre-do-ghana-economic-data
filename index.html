          How the live numbers are estimated

          Budget lines spread the approved 2026 figures evenly across the year. Population grows at the National Population Council’s projected rate. Dollar amounts convert at the Bank of Ghana interbank mid-rate shown in the economy section. Milestone dates assume today’s borrowing pace continues.

          External debt is the least certain estimate. Most of it is owed in foreign currency, so a weaker cedi raises it without any new borrowing and a stronger cedi lowers it. That is why the reported total dipped from GH¢720.8bn in May to GH¢719.5bn in June.
